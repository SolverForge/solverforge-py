## Completed cutover gates

The following criteria governed the atomic cutover completed by `de9355f`.
They are retained as the historical acceptance boundary.

- One upstream compiler graph and one provider/kernel executor must cover typed and dynamic scalar/list phases.
- The executor must freeze schema order, resolve public edit aliases before deduplication, and preserve Python exceptions through the binding panic boundary.
- Route/K-opt and Clarke-Wright savings access must remain independently declared at execution. A retained legacy flat declaration may normalize once into both immutable schema bundles, but no runtime fallback, coercion, or inferred equality is permitted after compilation.
- `solverforge-py` must delete `dynamic_scalar_search` list/scalar phase construction, TLS slot discovery, `PyDistanceMeter`, route metadata fallbacks, and synthetic defaults in the same cutover.
- The benchmark source checkout remains untouched. Only its isolated temporary copy may receive the Employee configuration alignment.
- Final measurements require release builds, CPU pinning, warmups, alternating paired trials, validators/fair-start checks, bounded-work counters, and fixed-budget quality statistics.
- Qualified paired records must additionally bind atomic terminal score and snapshot revision to a validated canonical final planning-state digest; a numeric cost alone is not outcome provenance.
